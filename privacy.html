Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 23, 2026

SparklyTurf ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share your personal information when you visit sparklyturf.com (the "Site") or use our services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, mailing address, and property details you provide when requesting a quote or contacting us.
  • Service Information: Details about your turf (size, condition, type), service preferences, and scheduling information.
  • Automatically Collected Information: IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, referring URL, and browsing behavior through cookies and similar technologies.
  • Analytics Data: We use Google Analytics to collect aggregated data about Site usage patterns.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our services
  • Respond to your inquiries and provide quotes
  • Schedule and manage service appointments
  • Communicate with you about services, promotions, and updates
  • Analyze Site usage and improve user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Information Sharing and Referrals

By submitting a quote request, service inquiry, or any contact form on this website, you acknowledge and agree that your submitted information, including but not limited to your name, contact details, project description, and location, may be shared with other vetted local professionals, contractors, or service providers in your area. This referral is made to ensure you receive timely, competitive, and relevant responses to your inquiry.

We do not guarantee the quality, pricing, or availability of any third-party professional to whom your information may be referred. By using this website, you consent to this referral process.

4. Other Information Sharing

In addition to the referral process described above, we may share your information with:

  • Service Providers: Third parties that help us operate the Site, process payments, or provide services on our behalf.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, subpoena, or to protect our rights, safety, or property.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Cookies and Tracking

Our Site uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience. These include:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for Site functionality.

You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect Site functionality.

Our Site may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When your data is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymize it.

9. Children's Privacy

Our Site and Services are not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will promptly delete it.

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Right to Know: Request information about what personal data we collect, use, and share.
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of your personal information, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Right to Opt-Out: Under Nevada law (NRS 603A.340), you have the right to opt out of the sale of your personal information. We do not currently sell personal information, but you may submit an opt-out request at any time.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your privacy rights.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@sparklyturf.com or call (702) 763-7363.
